# Taxonomy

Controlled vocabularies cover family, accord, note, occasion, weather, season, mood, luxury, projection, longevity, gender and collection. Terms have stable slugs, display labels, descriptions, parent relationships and active status. Slugs are immutable API identifiers; labels may be localized.

Stewards propose terms with definition, synonyms, examples and source. Review prevents near-duplicates and contradictory hierarchy. Deprecated terms remain inactive and are mapped to successors rather than deleted. Aliases handle external names without weakening canonical uniqueness. Fragrance-term weights express relevance from 0 to 1.
